> GLIMI is an experimental, community-oriented memecoin. It has no promised financial return, no guaranteed price, no launch date, and no token sale. ## 1. Overview

GLIMI is a meme and community project built around an original sugar-glider mascot wearing pixelated sunglasses. Its initial purpose is entertainment, online creativity, and community participation. There is no promised yield, staking return, revenue share, or specific utility.

## 3. Release and custody framework (draft)

- **Community (500M):** phased distribution over approximately 36 months under rules published before any program begins. Eligibility, abuse prevention, snapshot methodology and available amounts must be documented in advance. No airdrop is announced.
- **Liquidity reserve (300M):** release only when the project can fund the other side of a trading pair (for example USDC or SOL), with public disclosure of pool addresses, ownership and liquidity-position controls. A token reserve alone is **not funded liquidity**.
- **Treasury (100M):** preferably held under independently verified multisignature controls; expenditures, wallet addresses, authorizations and periodic reports to be made public before use.
- **Partnerships (50M):** staged releases for real, documented deliverables. No undisclosed promotion, misleading endorsement or artificial engagement.
- **Founders (50M):** planned 12-month cliff followed by monthly linear vesting across 24 months. The locking mechanism and beneficiary addresses must be published and verified before launch.

**Critical qualification:** All vesting, lockups, treasury controls and liquidity arrangements are currently **proposals**. They are not enforced by the Devnet prototype or any Mainnet contracts. Exact schedules and mechanisms will need separate review and approval.

## 4. Supply and permissions

A fixed maximum supply requires issuing exactly the approved quantity, checking the on-chain supply, then revoking the Mint Authority. Revocation should be confirmed on-chain and is irreversible for the applicable token authority. The project also proposes removing Freeze Authority so holders cannot be selectively frozen by the issuer, subject to review. These changes have **not** yet been executed on Mainnet.

The initial Devnet token is for technical testing only. Devnet authorities and test supply should not be confused with future Mainnet settings.

## 5. Launch prerequisites (no target date)

The project will not declare a live market or invite public purchasing until:

1. Mainnet deployment budget, account fees and funded liquidity are available from legitimate, disclosed sources.
2. Wallet custody and multisignature plan have been tested; launch and reserve addresses can be independently verified.
3. Token supply, decimals, authority removals and any release mechanism have passed technical verification.
4. Metadata and the official mascot image use resilient, appropriately controlled hosting.
5. Applicable Brazilian and other relevant legal requirements are assessed by qualified counsel before token sales or regulated services.
6. Public communication accurately discloses risks and makes no promise of investment returns.

No pre-sale, fundraising, whitelist, guaranteed exchange listing or launch date is announced in this draft.

## 6. Community commitments and risks

- No manufactured trading volume, wash trading, bought followers or misleading promotion.
- Clear separation between free community content and any future financial transaction.
- Public disclosure of team-controlled wallets and changes to allocation rules before any Mainnet release.
- No promise that participation creates token entitlement or monetary value.
- Memecoins are speculative and may remain illiquid, lose all trading value or fail to launch.
- Any planned rule can be revised **before** Mainnet deployment. Changes must be dated and explained publicly.
